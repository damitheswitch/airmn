# Code of conduct

AIRMN is a research community. Members and contributors should be able to participate without harassment or discrimination.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Credit researchers and sources accurately.
- Welcome people across disciplines, institutions, countries, and career stages.
- Handle disagreement through evidence and good-faith discussion.
- Protect privacy and confidential work.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, hate speech, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination based on identity, background, nationality, religion, gender, disability, or career stage.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Plagiarism, misrepresentation of research, or fabricated claims.
- Spam, deceptive promotion, or unwanted solicitation.

## Reporting and enforcement

If something happens in the Discord server, contact a moderator there. For this repository, open an issue only for non-sensitive matters. Do not post private reports, personal data, or confidential evidence publicly.

Community maintainers may remove content, reject contributions, restrict participation, or escalate reports when needed.
