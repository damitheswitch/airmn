# Community resources

This is a starter collection for AIRMN members and visitors. It is intentionally small. Contributions should favor stable, useful sources over long lists.

## Papers and research discovery

- [Hugging Face Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers) - daily machine learning papers with community discussion and upvotes.
- [arXiv Computer Science](https://arxiv.org/list/cs/recent) - recent computer science preprints, including AI, machine learning, NLP, robotics, and computer vision.
- [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/) - open archive for computational linguistics and NLP research.
- [OpenReview](https://openreview.net/) - submissions, reviews, and proceedings for many machine learning venues.
- [Semantic Scholar](https://www.semanticscholar.org/) - paper search, citation context, author pages, and research alerts.

## Deadlines and venues

- [AI Deadlines](https://www.aideadlines.org/) - countdowns and calendars for AI conference deadlines.
- [NeurIPS](https://neurips.cc/) - Neural Information Processing Systems conference.
- [ICML](https://icml.cc/) - International Conference on Machine Learning.
- [ICLR](https://iclr.cc/) - International Conference on Learning Representations.
- [CVPR](https://cvpr.thecvf.com/) - IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition.
- [IJCAI](https://www.ijcai.org/) - International Joint Conference on Artificial Intelligence.
- [AAAI](https://aaai.org/conference/aaai/) - AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence.

## Research tools

- [Papers with Code](https://paperswithcode.com/) - papers linked to code, datasets, benchmarks, and results.
- [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/) - citation search and researcher profiles.
- [Connected Papers](https://www.connectedpapers.com/) - visual exploration of related papers.
- [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/) - open repository for research outputs, datasets, and software.
- [Open Science Framework](https://osf.io/) - project management, preregistration, and open research outputs.

## Moroccan AI ecosystem

- [MoroccoAI](https://morocco.ai/) - a separate Moroccan AI community organization with research and education resources. This listing does not imply a partnership.
- [AIRMN Discord](https://discord.gg/w9jkvrNHWy) - the community's live discussion space.
