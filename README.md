# AI Research Moroccan Network (AIRMN)

**Connecting Moroccan Minds. Advancing AI Together.**

AIRMN is a global community for researchers, students, engineers, academics, founders, and professionals connected to Morocco and working across artificial intelligence and related fields.

This repository is the community's public home for information, resources, and brand material. The Discord server is where members meet, discuss research, share opportunities, and find collaborators.

## Join the community

[Join the AIRMN Discord server](https://discord.gg/w9jkvrNHWy)

## What happens in the community

- Research paper discussion and paper sharing
- Conference and submission deadline alerts
- Collaboration and mentorship opportunities
- Networking across institutions, countries, and career stages
- Community announcements and project discovery

## Repository contents

- [`resources/`](resources/README.md) - a curated starting point for research tools, paper sources, deadlines, and community links
- [`branding/`](branding/README.md) - the official logo and the meaning behind the mark
- [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) - how to suggest resources or improvements
- [`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) - the standard expected across AIRMN spaces

## Suggest an update

Open an issue if you want to suggest a resource, report a broken link, or propose an improvement. Please do not include private Discord information, personal data, or confidential material in public issues.
