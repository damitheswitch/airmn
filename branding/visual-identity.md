# AIRMN visual identity

**Working guide | 2026-10-07**

This guide organizes the proposed AIRMN visual system in one place. The logo and motto below are in current use. Layout colors, typography, and new logo variants are proposals, not approved production assets. Use this guide to prepare and review work; confirm those choices with the AIRMN organizers before releasing new designs.

## Identity at a glance

**Name:** AI Research Moroccan Network (AIRMN)  
**Motto:** Connecting Moroccan Minds. Advancing AI Together.  
**Idea:** Distributed globally. Connected intellectually. Advancing together.

AIRMN connects people with ties to Morocco who work in AI research and related fields. The identity should feel credible in academic and international settings: clear, geometric, restrained, and human. It should signal Morocco without imitating the flag or filling every surface with decorative patterns.

Aim for research, collaboration, precision, and welcome. Avoid startup hype, generic AI brains, robots, circuitry, glowing gradients, stock science imagery, and unsupported claims of institutional status.

## Logo and wordmark

The [current AIRMN logo](airmn-logo.png) is the reference artwork. It has red outer geometry and four nodes, pale paths, a green center, and a fixed AIRMN wordmark. See [the story behind the logo](README.md) for the meaning of those elements.

**Use this artwork as a single image.** Preserve its proportions, colors, wording, and clear space. Do not typeset a replacement wordmark, redraw the symbol, recolor the raster, add effects, or crop a symbol out of it. Place it where both mark and wordmark are legible. Alt text should identify AIRMN, not reproduce the full logo story.

The current Discord-derived file is a square raster with a light background, not a transparent vector or an approved dark-mode lockup. The proposed palette below does **not** exactly match its pixels. References to green inward paths and white connection strokes describe the concept; those paths are not separate, reusable layers in this raster. Do not paint them into the logo to make the artwork fit the prose.

Before creating dark, light, icon-only, horizontal, or transparent variants, obtain the editable master and export them with the identity owner's approval. Do not improvise variants from the Discord PNG. The [brand asset notice](../NOTICE) excludes the logo from the repository's MIT License.

## Color system (proposed)

These are proposed colors for layouts and supporting graphics, **not sampled logo colors**. Confirm them against an authorized master or with the identity owner before treating them as fixed brand specifications.

| Token | HEX | RGB | Suggested use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Red | `#CD161D` | 205, 22, 29 | Primary accent and supporting geometry |
| Green | `#00AA6D` | 0, 170, 109 | Sparing emphasis and convergence motif |
| Black | `#000000` | 0, 0, 0 | Primary dark surface |
| White | `#F8F8F8` | 248, 248, 248 | Light surface and dark-mode text |
| Graphite | `#171717` | 23, 23, 23 | Secondary dark surface and light-mode text |
| Slate | `#5D5D5D` | 93, 93, 93 | Secondary light-mode text, subject to contrast |
| Silver | `#A0A0A0` | 160, 160, 160 | Subtle metadata on dark surfaces only |

Lead with black and white, use red as the main accent, and reserve green for key signals. The source draft's 70/20-25/5-10 proportions are a rough composition guide, not a rule. Do not make every layout equally red and green.

**Contrast checks (rounded):** white on black 19.77:1; proposed red on white 5.30:1; red on black 3.73:1; green on black 6.97:1; green on white 2.84:1; slate on black 3.19:1; silver on white 2.46:1. For ordinary text, target at least 4.5:1. Avoid proposed red as small text on black, green as text on white, slate as text on black, and silver as text on white. Check final designs, not just token pairs.

### Light and dark applications

- **Dark:** black or graphite background, white text, red accent, green for a focal point. The current light-background logo is not a dark-mode logo; place it on an intentional light panel until a dark asset is approved.
- **Light:** white or near-white background, graphite text, red accent, green used sparingly. Prefer this for reports, letters, long-form reading, and print.

## Typography (proposed)

- **Display:** Space Grotesk. Bold 700 for major headlines, SemiBold 600 for sections, Medium 500 for short controls. Tight tracking near `-0.02em` can work on large Latin headlines; test it before widespread use.
- **Reading and interface:** Inter. Regular 400 for body text, Medium 500 for emphasis, SemiBold 600 for subheadings.
- **Arabic:** IBM Plex Sans Arabic or Noto Sans Arabic, chosen per project and tested with real Arabic copy and right-to-left layouts. Do not force Latin headline styling onto Arabic text.
- **French:** use the Latin display and reading pairing, then review line lengths and accents.

The custom wordmark inside the logo is fixed artwork. Space Grotesk is for headings, not a replacement logo font. If the proposed fonts are unavailable, use a readable system fallback. Check font licensing before bundling or redistributing font files.

## Layout, imagery, and graphic motifs

Use strong alignment, generous margins, and clear hierarchy. A 12-column grid can help a desktop website, but is not mandatory for flyers or social posts. A 6px or 8px corner radius can be a starting point, not a rule for every component.

Supporting graphics can use angular frames, four-point node structures, thin connectors, and a restrained center marker. These are **design motifs**, not permission to cut pieces from the current logo. Use one simple outline icon family with consistent stroke weight. Keep animation minimal and respect reduced-motion preferences.

Choose real, permissioned images of researchers, labs, conferences, whiteboards, academic spaces, and collaboration. Obtain consent where people are identifiable. Do not use photography that falsely implies AIRMN hosted an event or works with a pictured institution.

## Applications

- **Website and GitHub:** concise mission, clear join path, visible resources, and readable content areas. No invented metrics or institutional logos.
- **Event flyers:** AIRMN logo, event label, title, verified speaker and affiliation, date, time, place or link, then one clear call to action. Reserve green for one or two details.
- **Social formats:** repeatable treatments for research spotlights, publications, events, and opportunities. Vary imagery and hierarchy without losing the core style.
- **Reports and presentations:** prioritize readable text, precise citations, source credits, and accessible contrast over dramatic backgrounds.

Do not publish sample counts, researcher names, partnerships, publications, or event details as facts until verified. Sample website copy and metrics in the received draft were examples, not live AIRMN data.

## Voice and release checks

Write like a research community: direct, accurate, welcoming, and specific. Avoid inflated claims, excessive emoji, and marketing filler. Use the public motto consistently; campaign headlines are optional, not new taglines. Across AIRMN channels, use plain hyphens instead of em or en dashes.

Before a new visual asset is published:

1. Confirm that the mark is an authorized export and legible at its intended size.
2. Check text contrast on its actual background.
3. Verify names, numbers, dates, links, image permissions, and partner claims.
4. Have Arabic and French copy reviewed by readers of those languages when used.
5. Obtain approval from an AIRMN organizer and record the approved master asset.
